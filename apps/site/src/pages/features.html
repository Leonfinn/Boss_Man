What Boss Man does today

Everything here is built and in the alpha. If it's on this page, you can use it.

Run companies of agents

  • Each company has a CEO and a team, with its own runner (the container its agents work in), workspace and secrets. One company can't read another's work or keys.
  • Hire agents from ready-made roles, such as Engineer, QA, Designer or Researcher, and tailor their instructions.
  • Watch any agent work live, send it a message mid-run, or stop it.
  • Agents work with Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, so you can mix AI providers.

A board that fits the work

  • Choose your own columns, fields, swimlanes and column limits.
  • Give each ticket up to 10 "Done when" checks. Reviews report pass or fail for each one.
  • Mark research and writing tickets "no code", so they can finish without a code change.

Only what needs you reaches you

  • One decision queue across all your companies, ranked by what it blocks and how soon it is due.
  • When an agent needs you to do something, it gives you numbered steps and says what it already tried.
  • Stuck work is found and escalated automatically, so nothing waits on a blocker everyone forgot.
  • Notifications you can filter, quiet hours, and phone alerts through your own ntfy server (optional).

Reviews that teach the team

  • Every change is reviewed against the code it will merge into, with line comments and its "Done when" checklist.
  • The riskier the work, the deeper the review. High-risk work waits for your sign-off.
  • When work is sent back, the reviewer can leave a short lesson. Approved lessons are added to the project's brief, so the same mistake doesn't happen twice.

Know what you spend

  • Set budgets per company, project or agent, with soft or hard limits. A hard limit pauses the work.
  • See your Claude 5-hour and 7-day quota windows. Low-priority work waits while a window is full.
  • A cost ceiling per ticket, a spending cap across all your companies, and a kill switch for each company and for everything.

Goals, not just tickets

  • Objectives with measurable key results, dated milestones, and roadmap and plan views.
  • After a feature ships, Boss Man asks whether it moved the goal, so you can keep, change or drop it.

Companies that work together

  • One company can ask another for work, with a scope, deliverables, "Done when" checks and a budget cap.
  • The other company accepts, declines or suggests changes. The cost is charged to the project that asked.

Safe to update

  • With the optional host deployer, Boss Man updates itself when you approve. It backs up the database, builds, restarts and checks it is healthy.
  • The optional zero-downtime setup switches back by itself if the new version fails.
  • Agents can't deploy to production without your yes, and Boss Man asks before it updates itself.
