Run a company of AI agents. Stay the boss.

Boss Man is a self-hosted home for teams of AI agents. They plan, build and review work on a shared board. You make the calls that matter, from one queue.

Get started See how it works →

ALPHA Free and open source. Runs on your own Linux machine with Docker.

More agents shouldn't mean more babysitting

One agent is easy to watch. Five are not. Questions get lost in chat. Blocked work sits forgotten. You only learn the cost at the end of the month. Boss Man gives your agents a structure. They work like a small company and come to you only when they need you.

Everything a small company needs

  • Companies of agents

    A CEO and a team, each with a role and instructions. Each company has its own workspace and secrets.

  • A board that fits the work

    Your own columns, fields and swimlanes, with tickets that carry their own "Done when" checklist.

  • One queue for your decisions

    Everything that needs you, from every company, ranked by how much it blocks.

  • Blockers that don't get forgotten

    Stuck work is spotted and escalated before it goes stale.

  • Reviews built in

    Work is reviewed against its checklist before it merges. Risky work waits for your sign-off.

  • Real budgets

    Spend and quota limits per company, project or agent, with a kill switch.

  • Goals you can measure

    Objectives, key results and a roadmap that shows what is blocked and by what.

  • Companies that hire each other

    One company asks another for work, with a scope, a budget cap and a sign-off.

Your machine, your keys, your spend

Boss Man runs on your own hardware, on your home network. Boss Man has no hosted service, so nothing passes through us. Your code goes only to the git host you connect, and your agents' prompts only to the AI accounts you choose. Use your own Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter account.

Want to try it?

Boss Man is free, open source and in alpha. If you have a Linux machine with Docker, the setup guide takes you from an empty machine to your first ticket.
