## In short

Open **Decisions** in the sidebar. The number beside it counts the cards that need you now. Read the card, then press one of its buttons. That is all most cards need.

In your first days you get more cards than later. The CEO is your only agent at first, and a request from the CEO itself always comes to you.

## The kinds of card

The tag at the top of a card says what kind it is. The ones you meet first:

| Card | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Question** | An agent needs an answer to carry on, such as "Which of these two suppliers should the report cover?" |
| **Approval** | Yes or no to something, such as a hire the CEO proposed (**Hire** or **Don't hire**) or a deploy. |
| **Plan** | Whether an agent's plan for a high or critical risk task may go ahead. |
| **Permission** | Whether an agent may run a command or use a tool. See [Permission cards](#permission-cards-and-the-10-minute-wait). |
| **Sign-off** | Whether finished high or critical work may count as done. See [Review and risk](/guides/review-and-risk). |
| **Waiting for you** | Steps only you can do, such as a setting or a button. These come first in the list. |

Other kinds, such as **Budget**, **Stalled run** and **Boss Man update**, explain themselves on the card.

## Recommended, and Write my own answer

- **Recommended.** The agent marks the button it thinks is right. You do not have to press it. On plan, sign-off and permission cards, a line under each button says what happens if you press it.
- **Write my own answer.** On a question card, choose this when no button fits. Type your answer and choose **Send my answer**. The agent reads your words.
- **Not enough detail?** Choose it, then **Ask the CEO for advice**, and the CEO explains the card to you.

## If you do not answer

- **Most cards wait for you.** A task held up by the card waits in the **Blocked** column until you answer. If it is still waiting after 8 hours, the [watchdog](/guides/the-watchdog) reminds you once.
- **A permission card waits only 10 minutes.** After that the request counts as denied, and the agent has to find another way or ask again.

Nothing is lost by answering late. When a card is no longer needed, it leaves your list by itself.

## Permission cards and the 10-minute wait

A permission card asks whether an agent may run a command. It says in plain words what the command does and why the agent wants it. The buttons include **Allow once** and **Deny**.

If you will be away, you can stop the same card coming back: choose **Always allow this exact command**, where it is offered, or add a rule under **Settings > Permissions**. See [Permissions](/guides/permissions).

## Cards on your phone

Boss Man can send cards to your phone through ntfy, a free notification app. You need Tailscale and ntfy set up first: see [Phone notifications](/guides/phone-notifications).

Then open **Settings > Notifications** and find **Send to my phone**:

- **Urgent only (default)** sends only urgent cards. Permission cards, plans and hires stay on the Decisions page.
- **All decisions** sends every card that needs you, including permission cards. Choose this if you want to answer permission cards while you are away.
