## In short

Every company has one CEO. Boss Man creates it when you create the company, at the setup wizard's **Your company** step. It plans from what you type under **What it's for**.

You tell the CEO what you want. It works out the tasks, gives them to the right agents, checks simpler work and comes to you on **Decisions** when it needs you.

## What it does on its own

- **Plans the work.** It turns your request into tasks, each with what it should produce and how you will know it is done.
- **Hands out tasks.** It gives each task to an agent, or does it itself when nobody else can.
- **Rates the risk** of a task when you leave **Risk** on "Let the CEO rate it". See [Review and risk](/guides/review-and-risk).
- **Approves plans** for low and medium risk tasks, and **reviews** trivial and low risk work, by default. It reviews medium risk work too when the company has no senior developer.
- **Settles permission requests first.** When an agent asks to run a command that no rule covers, the CEO has five minutes to decide before the card comes to you. See [Permissions](/guides/permissions#who-is-asked).

## What it always asks you first

- **Hiring.** Every hire is a card on Decisions. Nobody is hired until you choose **Hire**.
- **Changing an agent's model.** Which AI model an agent uses, and what pays for it, always waits for you.
- **High and critical work.** You approve the plan before the work starts, and you sign the work off before it counts as done.
- **Spending past your limits.** When a spending limit you set is reached, a card on Decisions asks you whether to raise it. See [What agents cost](/guides/what-agents-cost).

By default, the CEO can change an agent's instructions without asking. You get a note each time, and you can undo it in **Agents**, then the agent, then **History**. To have those wait for you too, open **Settings > Team and CEO**, find **What the CEO can do without asking**, and choose **Hires, model and instruction changes wait**.

## How to talk to it

- **Chat.** Open **Chat** in the sidebar and choose the CEO. Ask for work in your own words, for example "Find three suppliers of recycled packaging near Leeds and compare their prices". The CEO adds the tasks and tells you what it added.
- **On a task.** Open the task and type in the box at the bottom of **Activity**. The message goes to the agent working on it, or to whoever the task is assigned to.
- **Let the CEO question you first.** On the **Add task** form, write a rough idea and choose **Let the CEO question me first**. The CEO asks a few questions, then adds the task for you. See [Your first company and task](/guides/first-company-and-ticket#let-the-ceo-question-you-first).

## How hiring works

The CEO needs other agents to hand work to. There are two ways to get them:

1. **Hire one yourself.** Open **Team > Agents** and choose **Hire**. Pick a ready-made role, such as Researcher or Writer, and give it a name. The steps are in [Your first company and task](/guides/first-company-and-ticket#hire-your-first-agent).
2. **Let the CEO propose it.** When work keeps waiting for someone with the right skills, the CEO proposes a hire. A card on Decisions shows the name, the role, the model, what pays for it and why. Choose **Hire** or **Don't hire**. The CEO can also propose a whole team on one card, and you tick the hires you want.

To change a hire later, open **Team > Agents**, then the agent's **⋯** menu, and choose **Edit**.
