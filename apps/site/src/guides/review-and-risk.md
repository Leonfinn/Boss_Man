## In short

- **Review** means another agent (or you) checks finished work against the task's Done when lines before it counts as done.
- **Risk** is how much harm a task could do if it went wrong. Higher risk gets more checks and your sign-off.

Most tasks are low or medium risk. The CEO and your other agents check those, and you are not asked. You are asked to approve the plan and sign off the work only for high and critical tasks.

## The five risk levels

| Risk | For example | Plan approved by | Work checked by | You sign off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Trivial** | Fix a typo in a wiki page | no plan needed | the CEO | no |
| **Low** | Write a one-page summary | the CEO | the CEO | no |
| **Medium** | A report a client will read | the CEO | a senior developer, or the CEO if you have none | no |
| **High** | Something that changes how your business runs | you | a senior developer, or the CEO | yes |
| **Critical** | Something that could lose data or money | you | a senior developer, or the CEO | yes |

The CEO rates a task's risk when you leave **Risk** on "Let the CEO rate it". You can change the rating on the task page. A task with no rating counts as medium.

This table is the starting point. To change who approves and who checks at each level, open **Settings > Reviews**. There you can also add extra checks, such as a security review.

## What a review checks

The agent that did the work reports pass or fail for each Done when line. The reviewer reads the result (a wiki page, files on the task, or code changes) and those reports. Then it approves the work, or sends it back with what to change.

## When you sign off

For high and critical work, a **Sign-off** card comes to you after the reviewer approves. It lists each review and its verdict, and how much changed. The task also shows in the **Your review** lane on the board.

- Choose **Approve and merge** to accept it. On the task page of a project with no code, the same button is called **Approve**.
- Choose **Send it back** to return it to the agent. Type what should change in the note box first.

## How to send work back

You can send back any task in the **Review** column, even while an agent is still reviewing it:

1. Open the task.
2. If it says it is waiting for an agent's review, open **Decide now instead**. Deciding now skips that review.
3. Choose **Request changes**.
4. Under **What should the agent change?**, say what is wrong, for example "The prices have no dates. Add the date for each one."
5. Choose **Send it back**.

The task goes back to **In progress**, and the agent is told what you wrote. When it is finished again, it is reviewed again.

You can also add a one-line **Lesson learned**. The CEO decides whether to add it to the project's brief, the notes every agent reads, and you are told the outcome.

If a task goes back and forth too many times, Boss Man stops it and asks you what to do.
